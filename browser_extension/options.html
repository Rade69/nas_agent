How to pair:
  1. Open Ricky's Settings → Browseri i kartice
  2. Click Poveži for your browser
  3. Copy the 6-character pairing code shown
  4. Paste it here, select your browser, and click Pair Now
  5. A green "ON" badge on the extension means it's connected

Privacy: This extension only reads tab titles and URLs for listing. It never reads page content, doesn't inject scripts, and doesn't track your browsing.
